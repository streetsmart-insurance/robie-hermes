# WOW Customer Service - Verification Methodologies
# All 7 Labels

> Rules marked **UNCONFIRMED** are inferred from notes or pattern analysis and
> require Alejandro's confirmation before being treated as settled.

## 1. Google Reviews
**Proof:** Google Business Profile review exists, rating counts
**Rule (Carlo):** "If they say someone's name, we give them the credit"
**Unnamed:** Match via EZLynx contacts, producer/CSR, aliases, spouses, business names
**Schedule:** 24th (ask staff), 27th (follow-up), 1st (reconcile, no 3rd outreach)
**Value:** $10 per verified review

## 2. Autopay Setup
**Proof (Carlo):** Carrier portal shows EFT/autopay enrolled ("carrier portal all day")
**Flag if:** Note says "paid in full" or "mortgage billed" (note screening, pending portal check)
**Flag if:** Note doesn't mention autopay/EFT at all (needs evidence or clarification)
**Value:** $10 per verified enrollment

## 3. New Customer CSR
**Proof (per Carlo):** New business reports, sales center reports, carrier downloads
**Flag if:** "App sent", "shell", "bound but not downloaded", "waiting on download" (pending report check)
**Flag if:** No policy number and no issuance evidence
**Value:** Per agency schedule

## 4. Cross Sell
**Proof (inferred — UNCONFIRMED):** (1) Client was already a customer, (2) New line/policy issued
**Check:** EZLynx for prior policies + new policy issuance
**Flag if:** BOR/renewal (not a new sale)
**Flag if:** App sent/shell (not issued)
**Value:** Per agency schedule

## 5. Coverage Enhancement
**Proof (inferred — UNCONFIRMED):** Coverage increased on EXISTING policy; three-enhancement rule UNCONFIRMED
**Flag if:** New policy (mislabeled - should be NCSR or Cross Sell)
**Flag if:** Request only, not completed
**Value:** Per agency schedule

## 6. Referral Won
**Rule (Carlo, 2026-09-27):** Referral = the agent GOT the referral — they wrote
a policy for a client, and that client referred someone else ("Hey, I want you
to talk to that person").
**Proof:** (1) linked applicant/client who referred, (2) Nicole's weekly referral
report (verified sending ~weekly; report tracks Referrer Link, gift card status)
**Flag if:** No referral link in note and not in Nicole's report (check with Nicole
whether missing from report or never a referral)
**Value:** Per agency schedule

## 7. All Star Call
**Proof (inferred — UNCONFIRMED):** (1) Call occurred (Magellan/Sonant record), (2) meets All Star quality standard (criteria UNCONFIRMED)
**Check:** Magellan call analytics (rule pending Alejandro's confirmation)
**Flag if:** No call record exists
**Flag if:** Call was routine (not "All Star" quality per confirmed criteria)
**Value:** Per agency schedule

## General Rules
- Labels are claims, not proof
- Employee notes are not conclusive
- Evidence must come from destination system (carrier portal, EZLynx, Magellan, Google)
- "Credits to add/reconcile" - not "missed payments" (payroll unavailable)
- Each label completed fully before moving to next
- Clarifications go to Alejandro, not Carlo
- UNCONFIRMED rules are screening aids only, never final rejection grounds

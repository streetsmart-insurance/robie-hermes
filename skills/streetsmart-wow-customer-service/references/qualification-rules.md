# WOW qualification and quality rules

## Universal quality standard

Qualifying work must be accurate, client-centered, owned, documented, and resolved. The record should show the request or opportunity, action taken, owner, deadline when follow-up was required, and outcome. An open issue without documentation, ownership, a deadline, and a next action is not a clean result.

Use no more than two client follow-up attempts unless the governing SOP is stricter. If the client remains unresponsive, document the attempts, notify the carrier when required, use Postal Mail when severity warrants, and close under the applicable SOP. Ask for reviews or referrals only after the service issue is resolved. Escalate upset clients, licensed coverage questions, underwriting uncertainty, carrier silence, or unclear ownership.

## Exact label matrix

| Exact label | Classification | Minimum verification | Incentive treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Account Round Quote` | Tracking only | A cross-sell quote was prepared and tied to the account | No payout |
| `Account Round Success` | Verified outcome | Completed cross-sell sale with policy/effective-date and carrier verification required by the current SOP | $50 account manager or $25 technician |
| `Referral Won` | Verified outcome | The referred lead became a paying customer; retain the referral-to-customer link | $25 |
| `Google Review` | Verified outcome | The current SOP's qualifying completion evidence. The walkthrough required finding the posted review in the approved agency review feed; a note that the employee asked is not enough when the posted review cannot be verified | $10 |
| `Coverage Enhancement` | Grouped verified outcome | Three qualifying coverage enhancements were completed and independently verified | $25 per qualifying group of three |
| `Coverage Enhancement-Technician` | Shared verified outcome | Qualifying shared enhancement work is complete and both contributors are identified | $12.50 each for account manager and technician |
| `All Star Call` | Verified outcome | A qualifying top-QA call or saved cancellation is supported by the authoritative QA/cancellation evidence | $5 |
| `AutoPay Offered` | Tracking only | Offer was documented | No payout |
| `AutoPay Setup` | Verified outcome | The insured signed the EFT form and the finance company or carrier confirmed setup | $5 VA or $10 agent |
| `New Customer CSR` | Premium-based verified outcome | New customer, issued policy/effective-date and carrier verification, qualifying written premium, and no cross-sell classification | Use the premium schedule below |

Do not add `AutoPay Setup` merely because a form was sent, the customer requested autopay, or the carrier portal was attempted. A carrier restriction, returned payment, or unresolved authorization remains `UNVERIFIED` until signed authorization and carrier/finance confirmation exist.

## New Customer CSR schedule

- $1–$3,000 premium: $50
- $3,000–$5,000: $75
- $5,000–$7,500: $100
- $7,500–$10,000: $150
- $10,000–$15,000: $250
- $15,000–$25,000: $400
- $25,000–$35,000: $600
- $35,000+: case-by-case management decision; never calculate automatically

Boundary values must follow the current authoritative incentive document. If adjacent brackets appear ambiguous at an exact boundary, mark the amount `UNVERIFIED` rather than choosing a higher payout.

## Cross-label rules

- Account-round and referral bonuses require a completed sale.
- Rewrites may qualify for coverage-enhancement incentives but do not qualify as `Account Round Success`.
- A later cancellation does not create a clawback once an issued policy was paid, under the current incentive policy.
- A single activity may carry several labels. Each label requires its own evidence and may have a different disposition.
- Do not award the same qualifying event twice because it appears in both a note and task-note row.
- Missing or incorrect exact labels are not repaired by this skill. Report them for authorized human review.
- New-customer and policy-premium checks are account-level verification tasks, not pivot-table counts.

## Exception handling

Classify as `UNVERIFIED` when evidence is missing, the label conflicts with the note, the employee is not on the approved roster, the client is unresolved or upset, authorization is unclear, a policy/payment status cannot be confirmed, or the exact SOP rule is unavailable. Include the missing evidence and the person or team who must decide.

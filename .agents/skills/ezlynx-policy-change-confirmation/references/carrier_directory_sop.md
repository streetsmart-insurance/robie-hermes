## 3. Communication Rules
1. **Never Email the Client on Carrier Endorsements**:
   - Only contact the carrier or underwriter to obtain policy change status or declarations.
   - Do NOT ask the client for carrier decs or inform the client about carrier internal delays.
2. **Internal Leadership Emails**:
   - When briefing team leads (Ashley, Sandy, Gabriela, Jake), always use clean, styled HTML with status badges, metric tables, and clear visual hierarchy.
3. **EZLynx Automation Center Coaching**:
   - Once a change is verified and closed in EZLynx, Automation Center automatically emails the client. No manual client email should be sent.

# Inland Marine & Equipment Floater SOP

## Line of Business Overview
Inland Marine covers property in transit, mobile contractor equipment, scheduled tools, computer systems, builders risk, and fine arts/jewelry.

---

## 1. Primary Verification Elements & Walkthrough References
- **Walkthrough Videos**:
  - Commercial Inland Marine: [Loom: Commercial IM Walkthrough](https://www.loom.com/share/421ff2e1099b4073b346d8a8e8fde402)
  - Personal Inland Marine: [Loom: Personal IM Walkthrough](https://www.loom.com/share/8e6e52245c744b3189b6e73ef0eb6cb3)
- **Key Verification Rules**:
  - **Schedule vs. Item Number**: In EZLynx, the Schedule Number is distinct from the Item Number. Verify both carefully.
  - **Scheduled Equipment Details**: Year, Make, Model, Serial Number / PIN, and Stated Amount / Limit.
  - **Storage Warranty**: Check whether property must be stored in a locked building or safe/vault (Yes/No).
  - **Builders Risk**: Total completed project value, construction phase duration, soft costs, and "permission to occupy" clause.

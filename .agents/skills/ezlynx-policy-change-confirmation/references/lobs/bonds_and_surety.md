# Surety & Fidelity Bonds SOP

## Line of Business Overview
Surety bonds guarantee compliance, performance, or financial responsibility (license/permit bonds, notary, title, public official, contract/bid bonds).

---

## 1. Primary Verification Elements & Walkthrough Reference
- **Walkthrough Video**: [Loom: Bond Walkthrough](https://www.loom.com/share/c3f9239853414b9abeed93f432f3edfb)
- **EZLynx Entry Constraints**:
  - For Commercial and Specialty Bonds where EZLynx lacks dedicated schedule grids, place the **Bond Limit, Obligee, and Exact Purpose** into the **Coverage Description Box**.
  - Match Obligee name and address precisely to the state/municipal license authority.
  - Employee Dishonesty bonds are entered under Property/Crime sections.

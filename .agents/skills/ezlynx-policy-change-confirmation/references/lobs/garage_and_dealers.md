# Garage & Dealers Policy SOP

## Line of Business Overview
Garage and Dealers policies cover auto dealerships, repair shops, service stations, and detailers. They combine auto liability, garagekeepers legal liability (direct primary vs. legal liability basis), and premises liability.

---

## 1. Primary Verification Elements & Walkthrough Reference
- **Walkthrough Video**: [Loom: Garage & Dealers Walkthrough](https://www.loom.com/share/2e53898e240a4036a0c8c63c2b796a9e)
- **Insured & Operations**:
  - Classify as **Dealer** (Class I employees: owners/salespeople; Class II: non-driving employees) or **Non-Dealer** (auto repair/service).
  - Business description and physical location with building details.
- **Coverages**:
  - Auto Liability (Symbols 21, 27, 28, 29).
  - Garagekeepers Liability: Comprehensive and Collision deductible, Limit per vehicle and limit per location.
  - Operations Liability: Premises & Operations.
- **Drivers Schedule**:
  - All regular drivers and mechanics must be keyed with DL #, state, DOB, and violation status.
- **Lienholders & Additional Interests**:
  - Floor plan lenders or premises landlords.

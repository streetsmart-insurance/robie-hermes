# Commercial Crime & Fidelity SOP

## Line of Business Overview
Commercial Crime policies protect organizations against financial losses from employee theft, forgery, alteration, computer fraud, funds transfer fraud, and counterfeit currency.

---

## 1. Primary Verification Elements & Walkthrough Reference
- **Walkthrough Video**: [Loom: Crime Walkthrough](https://www.loom.com/share/69a0c734285c466da1c11f9fcf889870)
- **Policy Setup**:
  - Select "Crime" line of business in EZLynx.
  - Verify property location with building records before saving.
  - Coverage basis: Verify if **Loss Sustained** or **Discovery** (most are Discovery; double check declarations).
- **Coverage Plans**:
  - Employee Theft / Dishonesty (Plan A).
  - Forgery or Alteration (Plan B).
  - Inside the Premises (Theft of Money and Securities).
  - Outside the Premises.
  - Computer Fraud & Funds Transfer Fraud.
- **Underwriting & Audits Tab**:
  - Internal financial controls, annual CPA audit status, dual signature requirements on checks, number of employees handling funds.

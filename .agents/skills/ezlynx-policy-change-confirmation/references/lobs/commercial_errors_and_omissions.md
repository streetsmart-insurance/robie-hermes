# Errors & Omissions (E&O) / Professional Liability SOP

## Line of Business Overview
Errors and Omissions (E&O) and Professional Liability policies protect professional service providers against claims alleging negligent acts, errors, mistakes, or omissions in the performance of professional duties. These are almost universally written on a **Claims-Made and Reported** basis, requiring meticulous tracking of retroactive dates and defense cost structures.

---

## 1. Primary Change Types & Required Data Elements

### A. Limits, Retentions & Defense Cost Terms
- **Policy Limit Structure**:
  - Each Claim Limit (e.g., $1,000,000)
  - Aggregate Limit (e.g., $1,000,000 or $2,000,000)
- **Retention (Deductible)**:
  - Per Claim Retention (e.g., $5,000, $10,000)
  - Aggregate Retention
  - Ancillary Deductibles (e.g., Privacy Breach Deductible: $1,000)
- **Defense Costs (Claims-Made Terms)**:
  - **Inside Limits (Defense within Limits / Erodible)**: Legal defense fees reduce the total policy payout limit.
  - **Outside Limits (Defense outside Limits / In Addition)**: Defense costs are paid in addition to the indemnity limit.
- **Retroactive Date (CRITICAL E&O GAP CHECK)**:
  - Must match the policy inception date or continuous prior acts date. Never advance the retroactive date on renewal or endorsement without explicit written client authorization.

### B. Revenue & Professional Service Scope
- **Gross Revenue Updates**: Current projected year revenue and prior 2 years' historical revenue.
- **Service Breakdown**: Percentage of revenue derived from each approved professional activity (e.g., 100% Precious Metal Appraisals & Trading).
- **Major Projects / Client Concentration**: Top 5 largest client jobs during the past 3 years.

## 3. Video SOP: Step-by-Step EZLynx Policy Entry & Endorsement Rules

> [!NOTE]
> **Source Walkthrough**: [E&O Loom Walkthrough](https://www.loom.com/share/5bc7644048f34c02a74ef8d4812c62bc) demonstrated on *The Millstone Mint Inc* (`0100240753-0`, Kinsale Insurance Company).

### Step 1: LOB Selection (CRITICAL RULE)
- **Select "Errors and Omissions"**: In EZLynx, **never select Professional Liability** for commercial miscellaneous E&O risks. Always choose **Errors and Omissions** so the correct ACORD 825 and financial underwriting screens populate.

### Step 2: Coverages & Financial Limits
- **Limits Schedule**:
  - Per Claim Limit: `$1,000,000`
  - Aggregate Limit: `$1,000,000`
- **Retention**:
  - Per Claim: `$10,000`
  - Aggregate: `$10,000`
  - Privacy Breach Deductible: `$1,000` (Privacy Breach Coverage Limit: `$15,000`)
- **Defense Costs**:
  - Separate Defense Limit: `No`
  - Inside or Outside: Select **Inside** if legal fees erode the aggregate; select **Outside** if defense is supplemental.
- **Retroactive Date**:
  - Key exact date (e.g., `05/18/2023` or policy inception).

### Step 3: Financial Information & Service Breakdown
- **Gross Revenue**: Enter Current Year Projected Revenue (e.g., `$720,000.00`) and prior 2 years.
- **Product / Service Description**:
  - Enter description: `Precious Metal Collectible Sales, Appraisals and Trading Services for Others for a Fee` = `100%`.
- **Top 5 Client Accounts**: Add top 5 largest client engagements from the past 3 years to document exposure concentration.

# Errors & Omissions (E&O) / Professional Liability SOP

## Line of Business Overview
E&O policies cover professionals and businesses against negligence, omissions, or failure to perform professional services.

---

## 1. Primary Verification Elements & Walkthrough Reference
- **Walkthrough Video**: [Loom: E&O Walkthrough](https://www.loom.com/share/5bc7644048f34c02a74ef8d4812c62bc)
- **Policy Setup**:
  - Select "E&O" (not generic professional liability).
- **Coverages & Defense**:
  - Limits of Liability: Per Claim and Aggregate.
  - Retention / Deductible per claim.
  - Defense Costs: Inside limits vs. Outside limits (Separate Defense Cost).
  - Retroactive Date: Must match original inception date or carrier warranty.
- **Financials & Underwriting**:
  - Gross revenues (current year + past 3 years).
  - Top 5 client jobs by revenue.
  - Specific professional service definitions in policy declarations.

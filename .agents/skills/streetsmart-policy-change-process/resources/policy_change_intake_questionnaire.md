# Policy Change Request Intake Questionnaire & Checklist (All Lines of Business)

This authoritative resource contains the exact questions, validation checks, and required documentation for every policy change transaction across Personal Lines, Commercial Auto, Commercial Property/Casualty, and Workers' Compensation.

---

## 1. Personal Auto Policy Changes

### A. Add Vehicle
| Category | Question / Intake Requirement | Field Validation & Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effective Date** | What is the exact effective date of the addition? | Cannot be backdated without carrier/underwriter approval. |
| **VIN** | What is the full 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number? | Must validate 17 characters; decode Year, Make, Model, Body Style, Trim, and Safety Features. |
| **Vehicle Specs** | Year, Make, Model, Body Style, Engine/Trim? | Match VIN decode results. |
| **Garaging Address** | Where will the vehicle be physically parked overnight? | Confirm physical street address, city, state, zip. Note if different from policy mailing address. |
| **Ownership / Title** | Who is the registered owner on the vehicle title/registration? | Must be Named Insured, Resident Spouse, or active listed driver. If titled to a business or other party, carrier approval required. |
| **Vehicle Use** | What is the primary use: Pleasure, Commute to Work/School, or Business? | If Commute: Number of days/week, one-way commute mileage. If Pleasure: Annual estimated mileage. If Business: Inquire about rideshare/delivery (Uber/Lyft/DoorDash) or artisan commercial use. |
| **Primary Driver** | Who in the household will be the primary driver of this vehicle? | Select from active listed drivers. If new driver, trigger **Add Driver** questionnaire. |
| **Coverage Desired** | Does the client want **Liability Only** or **Full Physical Damage**? | **Liability Only**: Enforce customer acknowledgement that Comprehensive and Collision will be declined. No glass, rental, or roadside unless specifically requested.<br>**Full Coverage**: Ask for Comprehensive deductible ($250, $500, $1,000) and Collision deductible ($500, $1,000, $2,000). |
| **Endorsements** | Does the client want Roadside Assistance / Towing & Labor? Rental Reimbursement ($30/day, $50/day)? Custom Equipment coverage? | Record specific limit options. |
| **Lienholder / Lease** | Is the vehicle financed, leased, or owned outright? | If Financed/Leased: Mandatory Full Coverage. Require financial institution legal name, loss payable clause, billing/notification address, and loan/account number. |
| **Evidence / Docs** | Do we have the vehicle registration, bill of sale, buyer's order, or title? | Save document to EZLynx account documents. |

### B. Remove / Delete Vehicle
| Category | Question / Intake Requirement | Field Validation & Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effective Date** | What is the effective date of the removal? | Date sold, traded, or returned. |
| **Vehicle Selection** | Which specific vehicle is being removed? | Year, Make, Model, VIN, and Unit number on carrier schedule. |
| **Disposition Reason** | What happened to the vehicle? | Sold, traded in, lease returned, totaled in claim, repossessed, junked/scrapped. |
| **Remaining Vehicles** | Are there other active vehicles remaining on the policy? | If deleting the only vehicle, policy must be cancelled or converted to Named Non-Owner policy. |
| **Replacement** | Is a new vehicle replacing this one? | If yes, process as a replacement transaction to preserve vehicle discounts and continuous coverage. |
| **Lienholder Release** | Was there an active lienholder on this vehicle? | Confirm loan payoff or trade-in so lender notification of cancellation is anticipated. |
| **Required Evidence** | Bill of Sale, Trade-in Agreement, Lease Return Receipt, or Junk Yard Receipt? | Required for backdated removals (>30 days). |

---

### C. Add Driver
| Category | Question / Intake Requirement | Field Validation & Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effective Date** | What is the effective date the driver is being added? | Date licensed or date moved into household. |
| **Full Legal Name** | What is the driver's full legal name (First, Middle, Last, Suffix)? | Must match Driver's License exactly. |
| **Date of Birth** | What is the driver's date of birth? | Confirm age. If youthful driver (<25), check Good Student Discount eligibility. |
| **Gender & Marital** | Gender and Marital Status (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed)? | Affects rating tier and household discount. |
| **Relationship** | What is the relationship to the Named Insured? | Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, Significant Other, Roommate, Employee. |
| **License Details** | Driver's License Number and State of Issuance? | Validate format against state DMV standards. |
| **Driving History** | Date first licensed? Any out-of-state license in past 3 years? | Check driving experience. Foreign license rules apply if <3 years US licensed. |
| **MVR Disclosures** | Any moving violations, speeding tickets, at-fault accidents, suspensions, or DUIs in past 5 years? | Carrier will run official MVR/CLUE report upon submission. |
| **Assigned Vehicle** | Which vehicle will this driver primarily operate? | Match driver to appropriate vehicle unit. |
| **Discounts** | Good Student (B average / 3.0 GPA)? Student Away at School (>100 miles without car)? Defensive Driving Course certificate? | Collect report card or course certificate if applicable. |

### D. Remove / Exclude Driver
| Category | Question / Intake Requirement | Field Validation & Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effective Date** | What is the effective date of removal? | Date moved out or date separate policy established. |
| **Driver Name** | Which driver is being removed or excluded? | Full name and driver unit number. |
| **Reason for Removal** | Why is the driver being removed? | Moved to new residence, obtained own insurance, married, deceased, no longer licensed. |
| **Proof of Residence** | If moved out: What is their new address? | Utility bill, lease agreement, new driver's license showing new address. |
| **Proof of Insurance** | If on own policy: Declarations page of new policy? | Must show driver as Named Insured or driver with active coverage. |
| **Driver Exclusion** | Is the driver remaining in household but excluded from driving? | **Mandatory**: Signed state-specific Named Driver Exclusion form acknowledging zero coverage if excluded person operates any vehicle. |

---

### E. Change Garaging Address / Moving
| Category | Question / Intake Requirement | Field Validation & Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effective Date** | When did/does the move take place? | Date of change. |
| **New Address** | What is the complete new street address, apartment/unit, city, state, zip? | Run postal validation; check new territory rating code. |
| **Mailing Address** | Is the mailing address identical to the new physical garaging address? | If PO Box, record both physical garaging and mailing. |
| **Commute Mileage** | Did the commute distance or route change with the new address? | Update one-way commute miles. |
| **Cross-Policy Sync** | Does the insured have Homeowners, Renters, Condo, or Umbrella with our agency? | Proactively offer to update or cross-sell property coverage at new location. |

## 2. Commercial Auto Policy Changes

| Change Type | Critical Questions & Data Requirements |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Add Commercial Auto** | 1. 17-digit VIN, Year, Make, Model, Body Style, GVWR (Gross Vehicle Weight Rating).<br>2. Registered Owner: Must be exact legal business entity name or DBA.<br>3. Vehicle Classification: Service (tools/equipment to job site), Commercial (delivery of goods/materials), or Retail (pick-up/delivery to retail consumers).<br>4. Radius of Operation: Local (<50 miles), Intermediate (50-200 miles), Long Distance (>200 miles).<br>5. Desired Coverages: Combined Single Limit (CSL), Stated Amount vs ACV, Comprehensive & Collision deductibles.<br>6. Additional Insured / Loss Payee: Lender name, address, certificate requirements. |
| **Add Commercial Driver** | 1. Full legal name, DOB, Driver's License # and State.<br>2. Commercial Driver's License (CDL) Class (A, B, C) and endorsements (Hazmat, Tanker, Passenger).<br>3. Date of hire and years of commercial driving experience.<br>4. Clean MVR confirmation (no reckless driving, DUI, or major suspensions). |
| **Delete Commercial Auto** | 1. Vehicle VIN and Unit #.<br>2. Effective date of deletion.<br>3. Reason: Sold, returned lease, vehicle out of service.<br>4. Certificate Holder Updates: Identify any certificate holders requiring deletion notice. |

## 3. Homeowners / Dwelling / Flood Policy Changes

| Change Type | Critical Questions & Data Requirements |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Mortgagee / Escrow Change** | 1. Exact new Mortgagee / Lender legal name and clause.<br>2. New loan number.<br>3. Escrow billing address and correspondence email/fax.<br>4. Payment method: Bill to Escrow / Mortgagee vs Direct Bill to Insured.<br>5. Effective date of loan transfer/refinance. |
| **Dwelling (Coverage A) Change** | 1. Requested Coverage A limit.<br>2. Reason for increase: Home renovation, room addition, new roof, square footage expansion.<br>3. Completed replacement cost estimator (360Value / MSB). |
| **Scheduled Personal Property** | 1. Category: Jewelry, Fine Arts, Furs, Silverware, Firearms, Musical Instruments.<br>2. Item description: Detailed gemological specs, appraisal date, appraised value.<br>3. Certified appraisal document dated within past 2-3 years. |
| **Deductible Modification** | 1. Current vs requested All-Peril deductible ($1,000, $2,500, $5,000).<br>2. Wind/Hail or Hurricane deductible (percentage vs flat dollar: 1%, 2%, 5% or $1,000, $2,500).<br>3. Signed deductible selection/rejection advisory. |

## 4. Commercial General Liability & Commercial Package (BOP)

| Change Type | Critical Questions & Data Requirements |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Add / Delete Location** | 1. Complete physical location address.<br>2. Operations conducted at this location.<br>3. Square footage occupied by insured vs leased to others.<br>4. Building construction (Frame, Joisted Masonry, Non-Combustible, Fire Resistive).<br>5. Fire protections: 100% automatic sprinkler system, central station fire/burglar alarm. |
| **Certificate Holder / Additional Insured** | 1. Certificate Holder legal name and mailing address.<br>2. Relationship to insured: Landlord, General Contractor, Property Owner, Municipality, Client.<br>3. Required endorsement forms: Ongoing Operations (CG 20 10 / CG 20 26), Completed Operations (CG 20 37), Primary & Non-Contributory wording, Waiver of Subrogation (CG 24 04).<br>4. Written contract requirement: Is there a signed contract requiring these coverages before work commences? |
| **Exposure / Payroll / Sales Adjustments** | 1. Classification codes affected.<br>2. Revised estimated annual gross receipts/sales.<br>3. Revised estimated annual payroll by employee classification (executive officers, clerical, field labor).<br>4. Subcontractor costs and verification that all subs maintain equal or higher limits with certificates of insurance on file. |

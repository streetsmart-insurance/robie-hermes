# StreetSmart Insurance: Yelp Lead Response Workflow

This skill defines the operational procedure and messaging standards for managing and responding to inbound consumer quote requests and leads via the **Yelp for Business** portal.

---

## 1. Operational Workflow

### Step 1: Open the Lead
1. Navigate to the **Yelp for Business** dashboard -> **My Leads / Inbox** section.
2. Click on the customer's lead entry (e.g., *Beverly B.*) to view the project/inquiry details.
3. Note key underwriting fields:
   - Coverage Type requested (e.g., Homeowners, Auto, Renters, Commercial).
   - Property / Risk type (House, Condo, Vehicle, Business).
   - Timeline / Urgency (e.g., As soon as possible, within a month).
   - Location / ZIP code (e.g., `08759` - Manchester Twp / Ocean County, NJ).

### Step 2: Initiate Response
1. Click the **Send price & availability** button located at the top of the conversation panel.

### Step 3: Set Price Range
1. In the "Provide an estimate" form, select the **Range** tab.
2. Enter the realistic annual baseline estimate based on standard New Jersey market benchmarks:
   - **Homeowners (HO-3 / Single Family House)**: Min `$1,000` | Max `$1,500` (typical NJ single-family home range; adjust upward for high coastal exposure or dwelling values > $500k).
   - **Condo (HO-6)**: Min `$450` | Max `$850`.
   - **Renters (HO-4)**: Min `$150` | Max `$250`.
   - **Personal Auto (6-month or annual)**: Provide appropriate market range or benchmark.
3. Click **Next**.

### Step 4: Confirm Booking Links
1. Review or verify the connected agency scheduling link (e.g., Calendly / booking URL).
2. Click **Next**.

### Step 5: Draft Custom Message & Send
1. Type a customized, personal reply in the text field addressing the prospect's exact location, property type, and timing.
2. Key messaging pillars to include:
   - **Local Independent Broker Advantage**: Compare 20+ top-rated carriers (Travelers, Plymouth Rock, Progressive, Chubb, etc.) to get optimal coverage at the lowest rate.
   - **Acknowledge specific details**: Mention the property type and town/ZIP code.
   - **Frictionless next step**: Offer a 5-minute phone call to `(732) 462-8343` or use the booking link.
3. Click the red **Send** button.

### Step 6: Management System Logging (EZLynx)
1. Add the prospect to EZLynx / Sales Center with lead source tagged as `Yelp`.
2. Key contact information and log discussion note for sales follow-up.

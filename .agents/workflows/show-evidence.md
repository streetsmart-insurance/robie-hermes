# Show evidence

1. Identify the requested Job, release, Test run, promotion, or rollback by immutable ID.
2. Read stored evidence and health results without exposing secrets or client data.
3. Show the status, commit SHA, release digest, environment, verifier, timestamps, checks, issues, confidence, and approved recording links.
4. Clearly distinguish action receipt from independent verification evidence.
5. If evidence is missing or non-authoritative, say `UNVERIFIED`; do not infer success.
